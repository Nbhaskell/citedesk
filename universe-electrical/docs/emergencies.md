# Emergencies and safety

## What counts as an emergency

Sparks, smoke or a burning smell from an outlet, switch, panel or fixture, an electric shock, exposed live wires, an outlet or switch that is hot to the touch, or water reaching wiring or the panel.

## How to reach us in an emergency

Call 555-0182. The line is answered 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays. An electrician is usually dispatched within 2 hours. This is not guaranteed.

## Emergency pricing

Emergency visits have an emergency call-out fee of $195 instead of the standard call-out fee. Labour and parts are charged as usual.

## Urgent but not an emergency

No power to part of the home, a breaker that keeps tripping, and an outlet that has stopped working are booked as the next available visit, same day if requested before noon and there is room.

## If you see sparks or smoke, or smell burning

If it is safe to reach, switch off the breaker for that circuit or the main breaker. Do not touch the outlet, switch or wires. If there is fire or smoke that will not stop, leave the building and call 911.

## If someone gets an electric shock

Do not touch a person who is still in contact with the source. Switch off the power if it is safe to do so, and call 911 if anyone is hurt.

## If a power line is down

Stay at least 30 feet away and call the power utility's emergency number or 911. Fernhollow Electric does not work on utility lines.

## If water reaches the wiring or the panel

Do not touch the panel, switches or outlets while standing in water. Stay out of the wet area and call the emergency line.

## If you smell gas

Leave the building at once. Do not use switches, lighters or phones inside. Call the gas utility's emergency number or 911 from outside. Fernhollow Electric does not do gas work.

## If a carbon monoxide alarm sounds

Leave the building and call 911.

## Power cuts

If the whole street has no power, the outage is the power utility's to fix; call the utility first. Fernhollow Electric works on wiring inside the home and up to the meter.
