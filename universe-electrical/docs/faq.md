# Frequently asked questions

## Do you charge to come out?

Yes. The standard call-out fee is $95. It covers travel and diagnosis, and it is credited toward the work if the customer approves the work during the same visit. Emergency visits and visits outside scheduled-visit hours have an emergency call-out fee of $195 instead.

## How much does it cost to replace an outlet or a switch?

Replacing an outlet or a switch is $120 each. A GFCI outlet is $165 each and a dimmer switch is $140 each.

## How much does an EV charger installation cost?

A Level 2 charger up to 25 feet from the panel is $850, including the permit, when you supply the charger. Longer runs, and a panel upgrade if the panel has no room, are priced by quote.

## Do you upgrade electrical panels?

Yes. Panel upgrades, including from 100 to 200 amps, are priced by quote after a site visit. The price includes the permit.

## My breaker keeps tripping. What should I do?

A breaker that keeps tripping is urgent but not an emergency. We find the cause and repair the wiring, priced as hourly labour plus parts. If you see sparks or smoke, or smell burning, if it is safe to reach, switch off the breaker for that circuit or the main breaker. Do not touch the outlet, switch or wires. If there is fire or smoke that will not stop, leave the building and call 911.

## Do you do plumbing or gas work?

No. If you smell gas, leave the building at once. Do not use switches, lighters or phones inside. Call the gas utility's emergency number or 911 from outside.

## Do you work on weekends?

Scheduled visits run on Saturday, with the first arrival at 9:00 am and the last arrival at 11:00 am. The office is closed on Sunday. The emergency line is answered 24 hours a day, every day.

## Can someone come today?

For an emergency, call 555-0182. For a problem that is urgent but not an emergency, such as no power to part of the home, we book the next available visit, and the same day if you ask before noon and there is room. No visit is confirmed until a staff member confirms it. A reply is never an automatic confirmation or a price guarantee.

## The whole street has no power. Can you help?

If the whole street has no power, the outage is the power utility's to fix; call the utility first. Fernhollow Electric works on wiring inside the home and up to the meter.

## Do you offer discounts?

No. We do not offer discounts, including senior, military, repeat-customer and price-match discounts.

## Do I need to be home?

An adult aged 18 or over must be present during the visit. The electrician needs clear access to the panel.

## Do you work on commercial buildings?

No. We serve homes and small residential buildings of up to 4 units.

## Do I need a permit?

Panel upgrades, EV chargers and new circuits need a permit. Fernhollow Electric applies for it, and the permit fee is included in the price or the quote.

## Will you help with my insurance claim?

Fernhollow Electric can provide an itemised invoice and photos for an insurance claim. It does not file claims or give opinions on coverage.

## Do you serve my area?

We serve Alderbrook Heights, Millrace, Saltmarsh, Tamarind Park and Wren Common. We also serve Dunmore Flats and Old Quarry, where a travel fee of $35 applies. We do not serve anywhere else.

## What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

You can reschedule or cancel free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice. With less notice, a fee of $50 applies.
