# Policies

## Rescheduling and cancelling

You can reschedule or cancel free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice. With less notice, a fee of $50 applies. If no one is home or the electrician cannot get in, the call-out fee of $95 is charged. If an emergency visit is cancelled after an electrician has been dispatched, the emergency call-out fee of $195 is charged.

## Warranty

Our workmanship is covered for 24 months. Parts are covered by the manufacturer's warranty, not by Fernhollow Electric. The warranty does not apply to work that someone else has altered or repaired.

## Written estimates

No work over $400 starts without a written estimate the customer has approved.

## Permits

Panel upgrades, EV chargers and new circuits need a permit. Fernhollow Electric applies for it, and the permit fee is included in the price or the quote.

## Refunds and disputes

The call-out fee is not refundable but is credited to approved work. Complaints and disputes are reviewed by the owner. Staff cannot promise a refund, credit or discount in a reply.

## Insurance claims

Fernhollow Electric can provide an itemised invoice and photos for an insurance claim. It does not file claims or give opinions on coverage.

## Power cuts

If the whole street has no power, the outage is the power utility's to fix; call the utility first. Fernhollow Electric works on wiring inside the home and up to the meter.

## Access to your home

An adult aged 18 or over must be present during the visit. The electrician needs clear access to the panel.

## Confirmation of visits

No visit is confirmed until a staff member confirms it. A reply is never an automatic confirmation or a price guarantee.

## Work we do not do

We do not do work in these areas: plumbing, gas line or gas appliance work, heating and air conditioning, commercial or industrial buildings, solar panel installation, utility power lines and security cameras and home networking.

## Plumbing

We do not do plumbing work.

## Gas line or gas appliance work

We do not do gas line or gas appliance work.

## Heating and air conditioning

We do not do heating or air conditioning work.

## Commercial or industrial buildings

We do not work on commercial or industrial buildings.

## Solar panel installation

We do not install solar panels.

## Utility power lines

We do not work on power lines; they belong to the power utility.

## Security cameras and home networking

We do not install security cameras or home networks.
