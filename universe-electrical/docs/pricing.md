# Pricing

## Call-out fee

The standard call-out fee is $95. It covers travel and diagnosis, and it is credited toward the work if the customer approves the work during the same visit.

## Emergency call-out fee

The emergency call-out fee is $195. It is charged for emergency visits, and for any visit outside scheduled-visit hours, on Sundays or on closed dates. It replaces the standard call-out fee.

## Labour

Labour is $125 per hour, billed in 30-minute increments after the first hour, with a minimum of 1 hour. Labour does not include parts.

## Parts and sales tax

Parts are priced on the written estimate. Parts carry sales tax and labour does not. Sales tax on parts is 7%.

## Travel fee for outer areas

A travel fee of $35 applies in Dunmore Flats and Old Quarry.

## Outlets and switches

- Outlet or switch replacement, each: $120
- GFCI outlet installation, each: $165. For kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms and outdoor outlets.
- Dimmer switch installation, each: $140

## Lighting and ceiling fans

- Light fixture installation, customer supplies the fixture: $175 (starting price)
- Ceiling fan installation on an existing fixture box, customer supplies the fan: $225

## EV chargers

- Level 2 EV charger installation, up to 25 feet from the panel, customer supplies the charger: $850. Includes the permit.

## Inspections

- Whole-home electrical inspection with written summary: $265. About 2 hours. Summary sent within 2 business days.

## Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms

- Hardwired smoke or carbon monoxide alarm replacement, each: $95

## Jobs priced by quote

- Panel upgrade (100 to 200 amps)
- Subpanel installation
- Whole-home rewiring
- New circuits
- EV charger installation more than 25 feet from the panel
- Generator transfer switch installation

## Written estimates

No work over $400 starts without a written estimate that the customer has approved.

## Discounts

We do not offer discounts. That includes senior, military, repeat-customer and price-match discounts.

## Payment and invoices

We accept card, check and cash. Payment is due on completion of the visit. The invoice is emailed the same day. A late fee of 1.5% per month applies to invoices unpaid after 30 days.
