# Service area

## Neighbourhoods we serve

We serve Alderbrook Heights, Millrace, Saltmarsh, Tamarind Park and Wren Common.

## Outer areas

We also serve Dunmore Flats and Old Quarry. A travel fee of $35 applies to these areas.

## Areas we do not serve

We do not serve anywhere else.

## Types of property

We serve homes and small residential buildings of up to 4 units. We do not work on commercial or industrial buildings.

## What we need to book a visit

Please tell us:

- service type
- preferred date
- property type
- neighbourhood or address area
