# Services

## What we offer

Fernhollow Electric offers the services below.

## Emergency electrical repair

Fast help for sparks, smoke or a burning smell from an outlet, switch or panel, exposed live wires, and water reaching the wiring. Pricing: emergency call-out fee plus hourly labour and parts.

## Panel upgrade

Replacing an old breaker panel or fuse box, or raising the service to 200 amps, including the permit. Pricing: quote only, after a site visit.

## Outlet and switch work

Replacing, adding or repairing outlets and switches, including GFCI outlets and dimmers. Pricing: flat price.

## Lighting and ceiling fan installation

Installing light fixtures and ceiling fans that you supply, and outdoor lighting. Pricing: flat price for a standard fixture or fan; new circuits are quote only.

## EV charger installation

Installing a Level 2 charger for an electric car that you supply, including the permit. Pricing: flat price for a standard installation; longer runs are quote only.

## Wiring repair and troubleshooting

Finding the cause of breakers that trip, outlets or lights that stopped working, and flickering, and repairing the wiring. Pricing: hourly labour plus parts.

## Electrical inspection

A whole-home electrical inspection with a written summary, for example before buying or selling a home. Pricing: flat price.

## General electrical repair

Other electrical repairs that are not listed above. Pricing: hourly labour plus parts, with a written estimate over $400.

## How to book

To book a visit, please tell us:

- service type
- preferred date
- property type
- neighbourhood or address area

No visit is confirmed until a staff member confirms it. A reply is never an automatic confirmation or a price guarantee.
