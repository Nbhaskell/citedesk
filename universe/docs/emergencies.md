# Emergencies and safety

## What counts as an emergency

A burst pipe, a leak that cannot be stopped, sewage backing up into the home, or flooding.

## How to reach us in an emergency

Call 555-0172. The line is answered 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays. A technician is usually dispatched within 2 hours. This is not guaranteed.

## Emergency pricing

Emergency visits have an emergency call-out fee of $175 instead of the standard call-out fee. Labour and parts are charged as usual.

## Urgent but not an emergency

No hot water, a toilet that will not stop running, and a slow leak that is contained are booked as the next available visit, same day if requested before noon and there is room.

## If water will not stop

Turn off the main water valve, usually where the water line enters the house or near the water meter.

## If you smell gas

Leave the building at once. Do not use switches, lighters or phones inside. Call the gas utility's emergency number or 911 from outside. Kestrel Hollow Plumbing does not do gas work.

## If sewage is backing up

Stop using sinks, tubs, showers and toilets until a plumber has looked at it.

## If a carbon monoxide alarm sounds

Leave the building and call 911.

## Water damage

Technicians stop the leak and repair the plumbing. They do not dry out, clean up or repair water damage; a restoration company is needed for that.
