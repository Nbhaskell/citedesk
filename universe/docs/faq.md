# Frequently asked questions

## Do you charge to come out?

Yes. The standard call-out fee is $89. It covers travel and diagnosis, and it is credited toward the work if the customer approves the work during the same visit. Emergency visits and visits outside scheduled-visit hours have an emergency call-out fee of $175 instead.

## How much does drain cleaning cost?

Drain cleaning for one fixture starts at $149. Main sewer line cleaning is $329.

## How much does a new water heater cost?

A 40-gallon tank water heater is $1,450 installed and a 50-gallon tank is $1,650 installed. Both prices include the permit and removal of the old unit. Tankless water heater installation is priced by quote.

## Can you find a hidden leak?

Yes. Electronic leak detection is $245, and it is credited toward the repair if the customer approves the repair during the same visit. Leak and pipe repairs are priced by quote, as hourly labour plus parts.

## Do you repair sewer lines?

Yes. Sewer line repair is priced by quote after a camera inspection. A camera inspection of a drain or sewer line is $175.

## Do you fix slab leaks?

Yes. Slab leak repair is priced by quote after leak detection, which is $245.

## Do you work on gas lines or gas appliances?

No, we do not do gas work. If you smell gas, leave the building at once. Do not use switches, lighters or phones inside. Call the gas utility's emergency number or 911 from outside.

## Do you work on weekends?

Scheduled visits run on Saturday, with the first arrival at 8:00 am and the last arrival at 1:00 pm. The office is closed on Sunday. The emergency line is answered 24 hours a day, every day.

## Can someone come today?

For an emergency, call 555-0172. For a problem that is urgent but not an emergency, such as no hot water, we book the next available visit, and the same day if you ask before noon and there is room. No visit is confirmed until a staff member confirms it. A reply is never an automatic confirmation or a price guarantee.

## Do you offer discounts?

No. We do not offer discounts, including senior, military, repeat-customer and price-match discounts.

## Do I need to be home?

An adult aged 18 or over must be present during the visit. Pets should be kept away from the work area.

## Do you work on commercial buildings?

No. We serve homes and small residential buildings of up to 4 units.

## Will you help with my insurance claim?

Kestrel Hollow Plumbing can provide an itemised invoice and photos for an insurance claim. It does not file claims or give opinions on coverage.

## Do you clean up water damage?

Technicians stop the leak and repair the plumbing. They do not dry out, clean up or repair water damage; a restoration company is needed for that.

## Do you serve my area?

We serve Alderbrook Heights, Millrace, Old Quarry, Tamarind Park and Wren Common. We also serve Dunmore Flats and Saltmarsh, where a travel fee of $40 applies. We do not serve anywhere else.

## What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

You can reschedule or cancel free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice. With less notice, a fee of $45 applies.
