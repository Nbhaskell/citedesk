# Hours and scheduling

## Office hours

The office is open Monday to Friday from 7:30 am to 5:00 pm and on Saturday from 8:00 am to noon. The office is closed on Sunday.

## Scheduled visits

Scheduled visits run Monday to Friday, with the first arrival at 8:00 am and the last arrival at 4:00 pm. On Saturday the first arrival is at 8:00 am and the last arrival is at 1:00 pm. Each arrival window is 2 hours. We call 30 minutes before we arrive.

## Emergency line

The emergency line, 555-0172, is answered 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays.

## Holidays and closures

- Thanksgiving Day: November 26, 2026
- Christmas Day: December 25, 2026
- New Year's Day: January 1, 2027

The office and scheduled visits are closed on these dates. The emergency line still answers and after-hours emergency pricing applies.

## How soon we can come

Standard appointments are usually 2 to 4 business days out; staff confirm the exact day.
