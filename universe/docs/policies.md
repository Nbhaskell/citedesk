# Policies

## Rescheduling and cancelling

You can reschedule or cancel free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice. With less notice, a fee of $45 applies. If no one is home or the technician cannot get in, the call-out fee of $89 is charged. If an emergency visit is cancelled after a technician has been dispatched, the emergency call-out fee of $175 is charged.

## Warranty

Our workmanship is covered for 12 months. Parts are covered by the manufacturer's warranty, not by Kestrel Hollow Plumbing. If the same drain clogs again within 30 days, one return visit is free. The warranty does not apply to work that someone else has altered or repaired.

## Written estimates

No work over $300 starts without a written estimate the customer has approved.

## Refunds and disputes

The call-out fee is not refundable but is credited to approved work. Complaints and disputes are reviewed by the owner. Staff cannot promise a refund, credit or discount in a reply.

## Insurance claims

Kestrel Hollow Plumbing can provide an itemised invoice and photos for an insurance claim. It does not file claims or give opinions on coverage.

## Water damage

Technicians stop the leak and repair the plumbing. They do not dry out, clean up or repair water damage; a restoration company is needed for that.

## Access to your home

An adult aged 18 or over must be present during the visit. Pets should be kept away from the work area.

## Confirmation of visits

No visit is confirmed until a staff member confirms it. A reply is never an automatic confirmation or a price guarantee.

## Work we do not do

- Gas line or gas appliance work
- Electrical work
- Heating and air conditioning
- Commercial or industrial buildings
- Septic tank pumping
- Well pumps
- Irrigation and sprinkler systems
- Mould remediation
- Roof leaks
