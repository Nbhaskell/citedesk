# Pricing

## Call-out fee

The standard call-out fee is $89. It covers travel and diagnosis, and it is credited toward the work if the customer approves the work during the same visit.

## Emergency call-out fee

The emergency call-out fee is $175. It is charged for emergency visits, and for any visit outside scheduled-visit hours, on Sundays or on closed dates. It replaces the standard call-out fee.

## Labour

Labour is $115 per hour, billed in 30-minute increments after the first hour, with a minimum of 1 hour. Labour does not include parts.

## Parts and sales tax

Parts are priced on the written estimate. Parts carry sales tax and labour does not. Sales tax on parts is 7%.

## Travel fee for outer areas

A travel fee of $40 applies in Dunmore Flats and Saltmarsh.

## Drain cleaning

- Drain cleaning, one fixture (sink, tub, shower or toilet): $149 (starting price)
- Main sewer line cleaning: $329

## Leak detection

- Leak detection (electronic, finds the source of a hidden leak): $245. Credited toward the repair if the customer approves it during the same visit.

## Inspections

- Camera inspection of a drain or sewer line: $175
- Whole-home plumbing inspection with written summary: $225. About 2 hours. Summary sent within 2 business days.

## Water heaters

- Water heater flush: $129
- Water heater replacement, 40-gallon tank, installed: $1,450. Includes permit and removal of the old unit.
- Water heater replacement, 50-gallon tank, installed: $1,650. Includes permit and removal of the old unit.

## Fixtures

- Faucet replacement, customer supplies the faucet: $165
- Toilet replacement, customer supplies the toilet: $210
- Garbage disposal installation, customer supplies the unit: $185
- Fixture shut-off valve replacement, each: $135

## Jobs priced by quote

- Tankless water heater installation
- Repiping
- Sewer line repair (after a camera inspection)
- Slab leak repair (after leak detection)
- Leak and pipe repair (hourly labour plus parts)
- Bathroom or kitchen remodel plumbing

## Written estimates

No work over $300 starts without a written estimate that the customer has approved.

## Discounts

We do not offer discounts. That includes senior, military, repeat-customer and price-match discounts.

## Payment and invoices

We accept card, check and cash. Payment is due on completion of the visit. The invoice is emailed the same day. A late fee of 1.5% per month applies to invoices unpaid after 30 days.
