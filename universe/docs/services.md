# Services

## What we offer

Kestrel Hollow Plumbing offers the services below.

## Emergency plumbing repair

Fast help for burst pipes, leaks that cannot be stopped, sewage backing up and flooding. Pricing: emergency call-out fee plus hourly labour and parts.

## Drain cleaning

Clearing blocked or slow sinks, tubs, showers and toilets. Pricing: flat price.

## Main sewer line cleaning

Clearing the main line that carries waste out of the house. Pricing: flat price.

## Sewer line repair

Repairing a damaged main sewer line once a camera inspection has shown the problem. Pricing: quote only, after a camera inspection.

## Leak detection

Electronic detection to find the source of a hidden leak. Pricing: flat price.

## Plumbing leak repair

Repairing leaking pipes and fittings. Pricing: quote only, hourly labour plus parts.

## Slab leak repair

Repairing a leak in pipes under a concrete slab floor, after leak detection has found it. Pricing: quote only, after leak detection.

## Water heater service and replacement

Flushing tank water heaters and replacing tank units. Tankless installation is by quote. Pricing: flat price for flush and tank replacement; tankless is quote only.

## Fixture installation

Installing faucets, toilets and garbage disposals that you supply, and replacing fixture shut-off valves. Pricing: flat price.

## Plumbing inspection

A whole-home inspection with a written summary, or a camera inspection of a drain or sewer line. Pricing: flat price.

## General plumbing repair

Other plumbing repairs that are not listed above. Pricing: hourly labour plus parts, with a written estimate over $300.

## How to book

To book a visit, please tell us:

- service type
- preferred date
- property type
- neighbourhood or address area

No visit is confirmed until a staff member confirms it. A reply is never an automatic confirmation or a price guarantee.
